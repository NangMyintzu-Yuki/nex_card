What we are selling

NEX CARD is a platform where anyone creates a shareable digital identity page — a digital name card, a personal portfolio, a business advertisement page, or a wedding invitation — and shares it through a unique URL and a QR code. Premium pages unlock NFC cards (tap to share) and premium templates, with a one-time payment in MMK and admin-verified proof of payment.

Target audience (Myanmar market first)

🎯 Audience 1 — Professionals

Engineers, designers, freelancers, salespeople, agents who hand out paper cards and want a shareable digital card + QR.

🎯 Audience 2 — SMEs & Shop Owners

Restaurants, salons, clinics, small traders who need a mobile business page, business hours, and promotions in one link.

🎯 Audience 3 — Couples & Event Planners

Engaged couples wanting a digital wedding invitation page with RSVP-style milestones and photo gallery.

🎯 Audience 4 — Job Seekers & Creators

Graduates and creators who need a portfolio page to link in their CV / TikTok bio.
