# NEX CARD — Sales Features & Myanmar Pricing
  
**Yearly fee · 20 templates · 4 categories · QR · NFC · Link**
  
This sheet is the **commercial plan**: yearly MMK fees so the product owner can cover development (spread over subscribers), domain/server, physical cards, printing, delivery, and marketing.
  
The live app still uses a **one-time** payment flow until billing is switched to yearly. These numbers are what to sell and to put in checkout later. Pre-order mode is still on.
  
Do **not** quote the homepage counters (“10K+ users”). Those are placeholder marketing. For sales, say: *new in Myanmar, paid yearly in kyat, page stays live while the plan is active.*
  
---
  
## Feature overview
  
NEX CARD is a **digital identity platform**. The customer gets a live page on a unique URL. They share it by **link**, **QR code**, or **NFC tap**. They edit photos and details anytime. The template they pick stays locked.
  
### What the customer gets (every paid year)
  
- Live public page at `nexcard.wetechmm.com/your-name`
- Mobile-friendly layout (works on any phone)
- Edit content anytime from the dashboard (name, photos, links, hours, projects, RSVP, and so on)
- QR code — generate and download PNG or SVG (128–1024px) for print, stalls, and Facebook
- Share on Viber, Telegram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, WhatsApp
- Page views and QR-scan counts
- SEO title, description, and share image for Facebook/Viber previews
- Email-verified account, light/dark dashboard
- Add the site to the phone home screen (PWA)
- Homepage helper in Myanmar and English
  
### Four page types (pick one template per type)
  
| Page type | Who it is for | What they can put on the page |
|---|---|---|
| **Digital name card** | Staff, sales, freelancers | Name, title, company, bio, photo, phone/email, socials, skills, colour, quote, **save to Contacts (vCard)** |
| **Portfolio** | Developers, designers, CEOs | Headline, bio, photo, CV link, available/busy badge, featured projects, work history, skill groups |
| **Business page** | Shops, clinics, agencies | Logo, hero photo, industry, services and prices, gallery, contact/hours, call-to-action button (a mini website) |
| **Wedding invitation** | Couples and planners | Couple bios, wedding date + countdown, love story, events, gallery, song, **RSVP** (meals, plus-one, deadline) |
  
One account can run **up to four pages** (one of each type). Each page is its **own yearly fee**.
  
### How they share
  
| Method | What happens | Included in |
|---|---|---|
| **Link** | Send the URL in Viber / Facebook | All plans |
| **QR** | Scan with the camera — page opens, no app | All plans |
| **NFC tap** | Phone on the physical card — page opens, no app | NFC and NFC+QR (year 1 includes the card) |
| **NFC + QR card** | Tap if the phone has NFC; otherwise scan the printed QR | NFC+QR (recommended) |

### Pay and stay live
  
- Pay **per page, per year** with **KBZPay**, **WavePay**, or **AYA Pay** (screenshot, admin review)
- If the year is not renewed, the public page should be taken offline (that is the rule to sell; wire it in the app when you switch to yearly billing)
- Content can change all year; the **template does not change** after they confirm
- Physical NFC card is included in **year 1** of NFC plans. Renewal keeps the **same URL and the same card**. A lost card is a reprint fee, not a new yearly plan

## Does a cheap one-time price cover the owner?
  
**No.** A one-time 15,000 / 39,000 / 49,000 MMK sale cannot pay server rent, ads, cards, print, and the build.
  
Yearly billing matches the real costs: **domain and server are yearly**. Development is recovered from **many years of subscribers**, not from one card.
  
| Cost bucket | Covered by yearly Digital? | Covered by yearly NFC+QR (year 1)? |
|---|---|---|
| Domain + server | Yes, at ~80+ active pages | Yes |
| Marketing (first year) | Built into year-1 fee | Built into year-1 fee |
| Physical card + print + delivery | N/A | Yes, if COGS stays ~15,000–20,000 |
| Development | Slice each year, not the whole build | Slice each year + year-1 margin |

## Owner cost model (planning numbers)
  
Replace these with your real invoices. Rate assumption: about **4,500 MMK ≈ 1 USD** (for overseas hosting quotes).
  
### A. Yearly platform (you pay this even with zero customers)
  
| Item | Planning MMK / year | Notes |

### B. Physical card (year 1 of NFC plans only)
  
| Item | Planning MMK / card | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| Delivery (Yangon) | 3,500 | Other cities: 5,000–8,000 |
| **Physical COGS** | **17,000** | Get three print quotes before you lock price |
  
Renewal years do **not** reprint the card unless they pay the reprint add-on.
  
### C. Selling cost
  
| Item | When | Planning MMK |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing / first customer | Year 1 | 8,000 (paid ads can be 15,000+) |
| Support + payment review | Every year | 2,000 |
| Retention / reminder to renew | Year 2+ | 3,000 |
  
### D. Development (recover over several years of fees)
  
| Item | Planning MMK | How yearly billing helps |
|---|---|---|
| Build / owner time to launch | 8,000,000 | Example only — use your real figure |
| If 100 pages × 3 years | ~27,000 / page / year | Spread across renewals, not one invoice |
| Slice in the prices below | 8,000–15,000 / year | Rest from volume and NFC year-1 margin |
  
**Year 1 — NFC+QR cost (100 pages):**  
17,000 (card) + 8,400 (server) + 10,000 (ads+support) + 15,000 (dev slice) ≈ **50,400 MMK**.  
Fee **69,000 / year** → about **18,600 MMK** left.
  
**Year 2 — same page, no new card:**  
8,400 (server) + 5,000 (support+renew reminder) + 8,000 (dev slice) ≈ **21,400 MMK**.  
Renewal **29,000 / year** → about **7,600 MMK** left, and the URL/QR/card stay valid.
  
**Year 1 — Digital QR cost:**  
500 + 8,400 + 10,000 + 8,000 ≈ **26,900 MMK**.  
Fee **29,000 / year** → small profit; this plan exists to grow the subscriber base.

## Before you publish prices
  
1. Get **written quotes** for NFC blanks, printing, and delivery. If physical COGS exceeds 22,000 MMK, Complete year 1 is **79,000**, not 69,000.
2. Put your **real development total** in the model (section D). Yearly billing is how you recover it — not a single Digital sale.
3. Do not sell NFC until fulfillment is ready (print file, stock, delivery days).
4. Template and category lock after onboarding. Say that up front.
5. Wire **expiry**: if the year is unpaid, unpublish the page. Otherwise a “yearly fee” is only a slogan.
6. Each of the four page types is a **separate yearly fee**.
