# NEX CARD — User Guide

Welcome to **NEX CARD**, your digital name card platform. This guide walks you through creating, paying for, and sharing your digital profile.

---

## 1. Getting Started

1. Go to the NEX CARD website and click **Register**.
2. Enter your name, email, and password.
3. Log in with your credentials. (Admins are taken to the admin panel; regular users go to the dashboard.)

---

## 2. Creating Your Profile (Onboarding)

From your dashboard, start the onboarding wizard:

1. **Choose a category** — Digital Name Card, Portfolio, Business Advertisement, or Wedding Invitation.
2. **Pick a template** — browse and preview designs. Some templates are **premium** (marked with a price).
3. **Select a plan** (premium templates only):
   | Plan | What you get |
   |------|--------------|
   | **QR Only** | A scannable QR code for your profile |
   | **NFC Only** | An NFC card (requires an NFC-capable phone to tap/share) |
   | **NFC + QR** | NFC card plus a QR code fallback |
4. **Upload payment proof** (premium templates only) — see Section 3.
5. **Confirm your slug** — this becomes your public link (`www.nexcard.wetechmm.com/your-name`). Slugs are unique.

> Free templates skip steps 3–4. You can start sharing immediately after confirming your slug.

---

## 3. Paying for a Premium Template

Premium templates are paid once, in **MMK (Myanmar Kyat)**.

1. After choosing your plan, you'll see the price.
2. Transfer the amount via **bank transfer or Viber**.
3. Take a screenshot of the successful transfer.
4. In the payment modal, click **"Click to upload screenshot"** (JPG, PNG, or WebP, max 5MB).
5. Click **Submit Payment**.

Your submission is now **PENDING** review by an admin.

---

## 4. Payment Status & Resubmitting

Watch the status badge on your dashboard profile card:

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Pending Approval** | Admin has not reviewed yet | Wait — no action needed |
| **Approved** | Payment accepted | You can now edit your profile content |
| **Rejected** | Admin declined (e.g. unclear screenshot) | Click **"Resubmit Payment"**, upload a clearer screenshot, and submit again |

When rejected, any previous rejected payment is cleared and you can upload a fresh proof with the same or a different plan.

---

## 5. Editing Your Profile

Once your payment is **Approved** (or for free templates), click **Edit** on your profile to:

- Update your name, bio, photo, and contact details.
- Add links (LinkedIn, GitHub, website, etc.).
- Change content anytime — your **template stays locked** once chosen.

Save your changes; the public profile updates automatically (cached for up to 1 hour).

---

## 6. Generating & Sharing Your QR Code

1. Open **QR** for your profile from the dashboard.
2. Click **Generate QR** — a permanent lock warning appears (your template/category freeze after this).
3. Download as **SVG** (vector) or **PNG** (128/256/512/1024px).
4. Share the QR code or your profile link (`www.nexcard.wetechmm.com/your-slug`).

When someone scans your QR, they land on `/p/your-slug` and the scan is counted separately from regular views.

---

## 7. Dashboard & Analytics

- **Dashboard** — lists all your profiles with publish/payment status.
- **Analytics** — view counts and QR scan counts per profile.

---

## 8. Account Settings

Go to **Settings** to:
- Change your password.
- Export your profile data (JSON).
- Delete your account.

---

## 9. Tips & Notes

- **One profile per category** — you can have, e.g., one Digital Name Card and one Portfolio, but not two of the same category.
- **Content is always editable**, but the template and category are permanent after onboarding confirmation and after QR generation.
- **NFC cards** require an NFC-capable phone. If unsure, choose **NFC + QR** for the QR fallback.
- Keep your payment screenshot clear and readable to avoid rejection.

---

## 10. Need Help?

If your payment is stuck or you can't edit your profile after approval, contact support with your profile slug and the email used at registration.
